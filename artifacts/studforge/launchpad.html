STUDForge Launchpad

A coin launch paired with a Roblox place.

Each launch records a ticker, a game prompt, and the fee rule. Creator fees on that coin are split 50% to coding, 25% to the creator, and 25% to buybacks of the original STUDS mint.

50%Coding. Pays the place build and later script updates.
25%Creator fee. Paid to the wallet that launched the pair.
25%Buyback of original STUDS.
